## Directive that shaped the night

> "we are rushing trying to do too much and not building the correct
> tooling/fundamental systems at all ... to edit/add a new settings tab...

game's own tab spawner, panel prefab, row templates and show path. PostFX, the
MODS tab and Controls->MODS are consumers of it, in that order, each proven
live before the next.
